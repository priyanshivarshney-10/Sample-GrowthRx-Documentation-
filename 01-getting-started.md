# Getting Started

## Introduction

Welcome to GrowthRx. This guide will help you get started with the platform and understand the basic workflow.

## Prerequisites

Before using GrowthRx, ensure that:

* You have access credentials.
* Your account has been activated.
* Required permissions have been assigned.

## Login

1. Open the GrowthRx application.
2. Enter your username and password.
3. Complete authentication if prompted.

## First Steps

After logging in:

1. Explore the dashboard.
2. Review available modules.
3. Configure your profile settings.
4. Familiarize yourself with key workflows.

## Need Help?

If you encounter any issues during setup, contact the GrowthRx support team.
