# Reports

## Overview

The Reports module provides visibility into campaign performance and operational metrics.

Reports help stakeholders monitor execution, engagement, and business outcomes.

## Available Reports

### Campaign Reports

View detailed performance metrics for campaigns, including:

* Delivery statistics
* Open rates
* Click-through rates
* Conversion metrics
* Engagement trends

### Schedule Reports

Automate report generation and delivery at predefined intervals.

Reports can be distributed to relevant stakeholders for regular review.

## Use Cases

* Campaign monitoring
* Performance tracking
* Stakeholder reporting
* Operational reviews
* Trend analysis

## Best Practices

* Review reports regularly.
* Schedule recurring reports for key metrics.
* Validate data before sharing externally.
* Focus on actionable insights.
