# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## What is GrowthRx?

GrowthRx is an internal customer engagement and analytics platform used for audience segmentation, campaign management, user communication, and journey automation.

## What are Segments used for?

Segments help group users based on their behavior, attributes, or activity. These groups can then be used for targeting campaigns and analysis.

## Which communication channels are supported?

GrowthRx supports:

* Push Notifications
* Rich Push Notifications
* Email Notifications
* In-App Notifications
* SMS
* WhatsApp Notifications

## What is a Journey?

A Journey is an automated workflow that triggers actions based on user behavior, events, or predefined conditions.

## What is the difference between a Trigger and an Action?

A Trigger starts the journey based on an event, while an Action defines what happens next, such as sending an Email, SMS, or Push Notification.

## What are Have and Have Not Filters?

* **Have Filter** targets users who have completed a specific action.
* **Have Not Filter** targets users who have not completed a specific action.

## Can users be targeted based on real-time activity?

Yes. Live Segments can be used to target users based on real-time activity and behavior.

## Where can I track campaign performance?

Campaign performance can be monitored through the Reports and Analytics modules.

## What analytics capabilities are available?

GrowthRx provides:

* User Profiles
* Events
* Funnels
* Cohorts
* Pivots
* Power Curve Analysis

## Who should I contact for support?

Please contact the GrowthRx Product or Support team for assistance.
